Simply Thousand – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Simply Thousand (1000) card game app for Android, published by Gamehound Games ("we", "us"). In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not connect to the internet. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions.

Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following files locally in its private storage:

  • your game in progress, so you can resume it;
  • your settings, such as language and display choices;
  • your game statistics, such as games played and won;
  • your progress in Challenges mode.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Third parties, ads and analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party services. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
